Magic’s 10 Best Alchemy Cards, Ranked Worst to Best

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Polygon · 2026.09.14Original language: English

Wizards of the Coast has scheduled the retirement of Alchemy in Magic: The Gathering Arena for early 2027; yet this digital-only format, launched in 2021, has brought designs into the game that paper cards could not easily support: cards can be adjusted in real time, and mechanics such as Conjure, Seek, and Perpetual are used, with the format even able to generate the Power Nine out of thin air. The article looks back on this experiment through 10 representative cards: Forsaken Crossroads directly addresses the disadvantage of being on the draw, while Symmetry Sage shows how the digital version can rebalance cards. The list is not merely a ranking from worst to best, but also a review of Arena’s most distinctive designs before Alchemy’s departure.
