Sakigarasu's Take

Queuing in the early hours and applying across regions is not proof that players have lost their reason; it is a rare experience showing that some works can still make people willingly put their time on the line. The criterion worth keeping is whether that commitment comes from a desire to draw closer to the work on one’s own terms, or from being pushed by a time-limited test and scarce access into proving one’s loyalty.
