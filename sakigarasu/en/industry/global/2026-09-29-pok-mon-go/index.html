Pokémon GO Harvest Festival: Key Points for Collecting Shiny Applin Variants (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Inside Games · 2026.09.29Original language: 日本語

Pokémon GO’s “Harvest Festival: Applin Collection” runs from September 29 at 10:00 until October 5 at 20:00, introducing Shiny Applin for the first time. Because Applin has three evolution paths, each requiring 20 Tart Apples, Sweet Apples, or Honey Apples respectively, and one path’s later evolution also requires setting a specific form as your Buddy and catching seven Dragon-type Pokémon, a Shiny Applin comes with multiple collection goals. Yuzumin, a player from Akita Prefecture, warns that Applin is a rare wild spawn and the required apple items are also difficult to obtain, so players should prioritize relevant opportunities on the map and make use of the branching Timed Research. If you cannot decide which evolution path to choose yet, the author recommends holding on to the path that can continue to a later form, since none of the current evolved forms is particularly prominent for Gym or Raid use.
