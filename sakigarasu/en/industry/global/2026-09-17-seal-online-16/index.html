《Seal Online》Adds 16 Third-Tier Classes and a Top-Level Dungeon (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

인벤 Inven（韓國，韓語） · 2026.09.17Original language: 한국어

This update to 《Seal Online》pushes both character progression and high-level challenges upward: Playwith Korea has added a total of 16 third-tier classes for 8 classes, allowing players to switch to the new classes after completing the corresponding advancement quests and challenge a high-difficulty mode based on the existing top-level dungeon. The newly added death-count mechanic and Abyss Bone gear exclusive to third-tier classes also create new directions for strategy and builds; at the same time, the experience required for leveling has been reduced to 70% of the original amount, while login rewards, growth bonuses of up to three times, and Chuseok quests lower the catch-up threshold and increase the incentive to log in during the event period.
