Cat Me If You Can Launches on Steam Today (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

루리웹 RuliWeb（韓國，韓語） · 2026.09.09Original language: 한국어

Cat Me If You Can, a 3D hand-drawn-style cat-finding adventure developed by the independent team The Cosmic Stag Games and published by Craftmos Games, has officially launched on Steam and supports Korean. Players will explore scenes set across different eras and cultural backgrounds in a world transformed into a black-and-white comic, using a camera to photograph the cats they find and gradually restore their colour. The full release features more than 725 cats with distinct appearances, some hidden in areas that require players to operate mechanisms, collect items, or unravel clues to access. It also includes more than 50 puzzles and quests. A 10% launch-week discount is available at the same time.
