Hearts of Iron IV’s Major Expansion Is Delayed as Paradox Adopts a New Release Approach (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Gry-Online.pl · 2026.09.06Original language: Polski

Paradox announced that it is delaying the next major expansion for Hearts of Iron IV because it needs more time to polish it. The previous paid expansion, Thunder at our Gates, received 70% negative reviews on Steam. Players had also criticised several earlier DLCs for being too buggy and for charging for shallow content that should have arrived as part of a free update. The studio said development diaries introducing the new DLC will begin earlier than in previous years so that player feedback can be collected sooner, while the concluding update for Thunder at our Gates will arrive on schedule. The current plan is as follows: update 1.19.3 launches on September 17 with Australian Underground and Indonesia and Siam content; Romania content will later receive a patch aimed at reducing clicks and information overload; and the next Expansion Pass, hinted to focus on the United States, will be postponed until after that content.
