Sakigarasu's Take

A day-one peak can only show that many people were willing to show up; it cannot answer for players whether the game will receive long-term updates, whether its servers can handle the load, or whether people can come back after leaving. If 120,000 players is treated as proof that the game has already succeeded, the time and testing costs paid by early players are eclipsed by a single momentary number; the safer view is to treat it as an entry signal, not a guarantee that the work is done.
