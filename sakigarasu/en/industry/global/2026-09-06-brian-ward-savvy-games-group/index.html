Brian Ward Confirms His Departure from Savvy Games Group (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Arab Game News（中東／波灣地區，英語） · 2026.09.06Original language: English

Savvy Games Group CEO Brian Ward confirmed his departure after fulfilling his contractual obligations to Savvy and its owner, Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund (PIF), bringing his five-year leadership tenure to an end. Bloomberg first reported the news on September 1, saying that PIF vice president and head of international investments Turqi Alnowaiser would become acting CEO. Ward wrote on LinkedIn that after more than 40 years of work, it was time to begin a new chapter and leave full-time employment, though he had been invited to remain as an adviser to the company. He moved to Riyadh in 2021 to establish Savvy, and during his tenure oversaw investments in and acquisitions of companies including Scopely, ESL, and FACEIT.
