Before the second server transfer, Inven reviewed the 13th Siege Battle. The focus was not a complete reshuffling, but how each war zone used its final clash to confirm the existing balance of power. In Astaire, the Orc Fortress changed hands several times after the battle began, but the original defenders ultimately held it; Pa'agrio maintained a distribution of two castles for one side and one for the other, with repeated probing by both offense and defense failing to produce a breakthrough.
