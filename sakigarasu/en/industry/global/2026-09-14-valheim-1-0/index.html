How to Defeat the Bear in Valheim 1.0 and What Resources Does It Drop? (translated)

JeuxVideo.com’s guide to the bear in Valheim 1.0 first breaks down the danger: the bear appears in the Black Forest, has high attack power, resists piercing and blunt damage, but is vulnerable to fire and can also be driven back by torches; its aggro range is relatively small, so players can use distance and retreating dodges instead of taking its attacks head-on. The weapons truly suited to the fight are a sword or fire, and swamp creatures can also be used to keep it occupied. Defeating it yields bear paws, bear meat, bear hide, and a trophy; the hide can be used to craft related equipment, while the complete bear set provides bonuses to damage, health recovery, and stamina, at the cost of low defense and increased damage taken. For players who only want to explore safely, this fight may not be worth it; those who want to craft the set are the ones who need to prepare according to the guide.
