Jujutsu Kaisen Rumble: Survivaton Delayed to 2027 (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

4Gamers Thailand（泰國，泰語） · 2026.09.17Original language: th

Jujutsu Kaisen Rumble: Survivaton is a 2D action game similar to Vampire Survivors, in which players move around, defeat monsters, collect items, and improve their characters’ abilities, while also being able to use characters from the Jujutsu Kaisen anime; the game supports co-op for up to eight players and also adds mechanics involving accumulating points, casting “curses” to interfere with opponents, and challenging opponents to compete for points. Originally scheduled for release in 2026, the game has now been moved to 2027; developer poncle said the delay is intended to bring the player experience to an ideal state and was also affected by multiple unfavorable conditions. Following the delay, the game is slated for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, and Steam, so players’ original expectations for 2026 must now be replanned around the new release schedule.
