Heartstop Town Brings Its Back-to-School Charity Initiative to a Rural School (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

遊研社 · 2026.09.07Original language: 中文

On September 4, Heartstop Town launched the charitable education-support initiative “A Token of Care from the Town,” bringing the spirit of mutual aid among neighbours from the game to Dayaosao Primary School in Nanping Town, Guangnan County, Wenshan Prefecture, Yunnan Province. The team donated desks and chairs, daily necessities for students, basketballs, badminton equipment and art supplies, and also arranged sports activities and art lessons to respond to rural children’s needs in learning, daily life and emotional companionship.
