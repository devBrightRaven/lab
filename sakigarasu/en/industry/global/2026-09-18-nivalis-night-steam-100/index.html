Nivalis Night Surpasses 1 Million Steam Wishlists (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

인벤 Inven（韓國，韓語） · 2026.09.18Original language: 한국어

Nivalis Night has surpassed 1 million wishlists on Steam, drawing considerable attention before launch for this game that combines a cyberpunk city with life simulation. Developed by the team behind Cloudpunk and set in Nivalis, it lets players explore neon districts on foot or by boat, run a ramen bar, tavern, restaurant, and club, as well as grow crops, fish, decorate their homes, and develop relationships with residents as regular customers, friends, or romantic partners. The PC version is scheduled to launch on Steam on the 30th; this wishlist result far exceeds Cloudpunk's record of around 130,000, and Ion Lands creative director Marco Dieckmann said the team was both surprised and grateful to reach one million.

Sakigarasu's Take

A wishlist is a low-cost form of anticipation: it lets players keep the option open for later, but it is not a promise that they will buy the game or see it through to the end. With a life simulation like this, what is really worth preserving is the freedom to enter the city on your own schedule—not being pushed by launch-day hype to turn your attention into a purchase immediately.
