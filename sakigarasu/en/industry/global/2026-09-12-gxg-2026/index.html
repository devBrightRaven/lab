GXG 2026 Game Culture Festival Opens in Seongnam (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

인벤 Inven（韓國，韓語） · 2026.09.11Original language: 한국어

GXG 2026 is being held from September 11 to 12 around Banpo Station Plaza and Tech One Tower. The opening ceremony on the first day is hosted by Seongnam City and co-hosted by the Seongnam Industry Promotion Agency and the Game Culture Foundation, with a celebratory performance by Dynamic Duo. The event offers not only gaming and creative experiences, but also a range of exhibitions and stage programmes. The second day will also feature the AI GSDC awards ceremony and a concert, bringing games, creators, and audiences together in the same space.
