FEEDSTOCK Reveals Its Steam Store Page: The More You Carry, the Bigger Your Partner Gets—and the Harder It Is to Retreat (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

4Gamer.net · 2026.09.06Original language: 日本語

Ahaha studio revealed FEEDSTOCK’s Steam store page on September 6, with a planned release in October 2026. It is a first-person cooperative horror game for up to four players. Players feed recovered items to their companion, Tally, in exchange for materials, which can then be sold to expand their equipment; but the more Tally eats, the larger it becomes, so a door it could pass through on the way in may be impossible to pass through on the way back. Making it spit out some of the items also costs corresponding resources. The game supports proximity voice chat and requires a microphone because Tally follows players who are speaking. There are four types of enemies inside, each responding to a different noise threshold. The game provides no weapons, and enemies cannot be knocked down.
