AMD Adrenalin 26.9.1 Fixes Crashes in Apex Legends and Starfield (translated)

Jagat Play（印尼，印尼語） · 2026.09.06Original language: id

AMD has released the Adrenalin 26.9.1 driver. In addition to supporting Onimusha: Way of the Sword and The Blood of Dawnwalker, it also fixes potential crashes affecting the Radeon RX 9000 series in Apex Legends and Starfield. The update also addresses Smart Access Memory potentially being disabled after installing Windows 10, as well as an incorrect Anti-Lag 2 display status in Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy. However, RX 9050 4GB players are still advised to lower or disable graphics features that place a heavy load on the card when playing Cyberpunk 2077.
