The Founders of Two Point Studios, Now in Their Sixties, Are Still Preparing the “Next Generation” of Two Point (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

遊研社 · 2026.09.28Original language: 中文

As Two Point Studios marked its 10th anniversary, Mark Webley and Gary Carr introduced the Two Point Museum DLC Lost Amusement Park at an event in Shanghai on September 13. The preview announced that the content would bring a drop tower, pirate ship, Ferris wheel, and roller coaster into museum management; players will also be able to design tracks themselves and place the attractions in existing museums. A preview released on September 16 showed that the DLC continues the studio’s direction of turning new themes into new systems rather than merely adding decorations. The two founders also said that the team is still making another DLC, that the next game has entered development, and that an even earlier concept is being prototyped. The studio is therefore expanding Two Point Museum, developing a new team, and handing its focus to the next challenge at the same time.
