Sakigarasu's Take

If games can genuinely move beyond the original entry point, players will have more ways to access them, and creators will no longer have to stake every opportunity for reach on a single platform. The trade-off is that account systems, content governance, and revenue rules may become more complicated; having more choices does not necessarily make it easier to leave.
