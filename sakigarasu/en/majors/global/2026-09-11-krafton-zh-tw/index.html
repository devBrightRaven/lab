Krafton Named a Korea Best Workplace for the Fifth Time en (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

인벤 Inven（韓國，韓語） · 2026.09.11Original language: 한국어

Krafton has been named a “Korea Best Workplace” for the fifth time, while also presenting its family-friendly policies and investment in an AI First transformation. Since 2025, Krafton has expanded support for childbirth and childcare, including career support of up to KRW 100 million per child, parental leave of up to two years, and work-from-home arrangements for employees caring for their children. The company also said that, starting in 2026, it will allocate approximately KRW 30 billion annually to support AI tools and training. Han So-young, head of HR, said this represents the results of improving both the quantity and quality of work.
