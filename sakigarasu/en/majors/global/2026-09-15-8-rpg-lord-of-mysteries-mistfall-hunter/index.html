Livestreaming Tracker August 2026 Highlights: RPGs Lead China’s Livestreaming Rankings While Lord of Mysteries and Mistfall Hunter Gain Traction

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Niko News by Niko Partners · 2026.09.14Original language: English

Niko Partners’ August 2026 China game livestream tracker shows that RPGs remain the most-watched genre, with major updates and preview livestreams continuing to draw audiences. The new title Lord of Mysteries debuted at No. 16 in the RPG category, accumulating 5.6 million views across Huya, Douyu, and Bilibili, while Mistfall Hunter entered the same ranking at No. 24 with 2 million views. After the former launched on August 21, it added 1 million registrations in its first hour, surpassed 5 million within 24 hours, and generated more than US$20 million in revenue across all platforms in its first week; the latter sold 1 million copies in its first week. Both titles drew attention for features such as their visuals or combat, while also facing criticism over monetization, servers, or system design. Whether livestream exposure can turn into a lasting audience still depends on whether the momentum continues.

Sakigarasu's Take

Livestreaming charts turn players’ attention into comparable numbers, but they can also make “watched more” come before “is it right for me,” effectively screening games on people’s behalf. For new releases, that exposure is an entry point; for players, keeping the freedom to choose and walk away protects their time better than chasing the rankings to catch up.
