Australian Players Can Use McDonald's Rewards Points to Play the World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Early (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Press Start（澳洲，英語） · 2026.09.18Original language: English

Australian players can use 1,000 MyMaccas Rewards points to redeem an early-access code for the World of Warcraft: Forever beta through Partner Rewards in the McDonald's app; the beta began on September 17, 2026 AEST and runs until October 22. The redemption deadline is 11:59 p.m. AEST on October 20, and supplies are limited; after obtaining a code, players must also log in to or create a Battle.net account to activate it. Those without enough points can still pre-order the $96.00 Skyborne Epic Pack for guaranteed eligibility, or register for a chance to gain access. This partnership places an early route into the game test inside Australia's food-service rewards system, allowing players to choose among redeeming points, pre-ordering guaranteed eligibility, and registering for a chance to qualify.

Putting early-access rights into food-service rewards points adds another entry route, but it also makes the timing of play depend on cross-brand redemption conditions.
