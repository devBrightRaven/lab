World of Warcraft: Forever is scheduled to launch on November 4, with a beta opening first on September 17. Those who want to enter early can purchase early-access eligibility, and it will stand alongside the standard version and Classic as the third way to play World of Warcraft. Blizzard positions it as “a new beginning”: players will explore new areas such as Mount Hyjal and Riverlands in an Azeroth different from the one they remember, and can choose Forsaken Paladin, Dwarf Shaman, and the new Skyborne race when creating a character. The content also includes new quests, more group-oriented areas, and a permanent level-60 cap. The studio hopes players will progress at their own pace and will continue updating content across all level ranges after launch, allowing this new path to serve both players who want to tackle group content and those who simply want to explore the world.
