Miami-Dade County Wants to Market the City Through GTA 6, but Faces Opposition from the Sheriff and Council Members (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

4Gamers · 2026.09.06Original language: 中文

GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19, and Miami-Dade County had initially envisioned working with Rockstar to give airport baggage carousels, welcome signs, Metromover trains, and manhole covers a game-inspired makeover, thereby attracting players eager to visit and boosting tourism. However, Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz, council member Juan Carlos Bermudez, and judicial authorities oppose having the public sector promote a game depicting crime and violence, while the county government says the partnership has not yet been formally approved. Meanwhile, the City of Miami is preparing to vote on a temporary ordinance on September 10. If passed, it would relax advertising regulations from October 15 through December 31, allowing Rockstar to compete for six advertising sites in the city center and bringing an estimated $1.2 million-plus to the city treasury; it remains uncertain whether the ordinance will pass.

Local governments can borrow the game’s attention, while players have no control over how their cultural experience is repackaged.
