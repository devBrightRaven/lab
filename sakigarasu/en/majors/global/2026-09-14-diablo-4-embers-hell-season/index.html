Diablo 30th Anniversary: “Embers of Hell” Season Launches Sept. 16! Battle Diablo, Baal, Mephisto, and Other Series Bosses (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

4Gamers · 2026.09.14Original language: 中文

Diablo IV will launch the “Embers of Hell” Season on September 16, turning nostalgic content for the series’ 30th anniversary into a replayable season and permanent activities. At a BlizzCon panel, the development team explained that echoes of Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto will return to Sanctuary alongside familiar faces such as Deckard Cain and Nihlathak; players will also be able to challenge classic locations from the original game through Diablo Immortal, encounter nearly 20 bosses from across the series and more than 25 characters, and obtain nine classic Unique items that will remain permanently. The seasonal quests continue the story of the death cult and the dead’s transformation, while Evil Shards, reborn characters, and an account-wide Conquest plan reshape progression. The Horadric Cube and occult crafting are also being adjusted, allowing existing gear to enter a new progression path; as a result, this is not merely a retrospective, but also connects the series’ memories to the current endgame and future story.
