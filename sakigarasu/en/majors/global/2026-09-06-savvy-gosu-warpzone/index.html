Savvy Signs Memoranda of Understanding with Gosu and Warpzone (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Arab Game News（中東／波灣地區，英語） · 2026.09.06Original language: English

During LEAP 2026 in Riyadh, Savvy Games Group signed two memoranda of understanding with Gosu Academy and Warpzone AI Labs. The agreement with Gosu Academy explores education in games and esports, including employment preparation, experiential and gamified learning, and simulated training. The agreement with Warpzone AI Labs focuses on knowledge transfer and mentorship for AI-native game development, and the parties will also assess establishing a local R&D lab dedicated to developing and testing AI-native games. Both memoranda emphasise building local skills and expertise. They remain exploratory, with no specific courses, jobs, or products confirmed yet.
