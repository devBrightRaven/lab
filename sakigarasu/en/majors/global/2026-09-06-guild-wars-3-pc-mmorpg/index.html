Guild Wars 3 appeared at Gamescom 2026 in a roughly 20-minute hands-off preview, showcasing movement and combat systems that are still in the pre-alpha stage. The preview placed particular emphasis on three-dimensional terrain, stamina-free climbing and gliding, and momentum that affects attack damage; in co-op, a warrior’s shield can serve as a springboard for teammates, while a necromancer can add a fiery effect to the party’s weapons.
