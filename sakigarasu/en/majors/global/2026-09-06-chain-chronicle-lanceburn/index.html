Chain Chronicle Adds Lancelot, Who Has Obtained the Power of the Otherworldly God (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

4Gamer.net · 2026.09.06Original language: 日本語

Chain Chronicle will hold the “Otherworldly God Liberation Festival” from September 6 at 11:00 to September 15 at 10:59. The featured character is Lancelot, who has obtained the power of the otherworldly god, voiced by Ryotaro Okiayu, and he can be acquired through a limited-time gacha. The official announcement also detailed the pull-reward ladder: 10 pulls grant a premium ticket; starting at 20 and 30 pulls, free ten-pulls and a guaranteed SSR are unlocked in succession; 100 pulls directly grant the character; and 120 and 150 pulls provide additional SSR exchange rewards. During the event, the game will also offer a trial quest for the new character, training quests, daily weapon-material quests, and reruns of the “Chronicle’s Awakening” characters and event quests featuring Lancelot.
