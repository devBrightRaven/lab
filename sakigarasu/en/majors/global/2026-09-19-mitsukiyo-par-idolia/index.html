Mitsukiyo on Paréidolia: The Musical Core Where Comfort and Mystery Coexist (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

인벤 Inven（韓國，韓語） · 2026.09.19Original language: 한국어

At TGS 2026, Nexon Games' IO division officially unveiled Paréidolia, previously known as Project RX, and disclosed actual gameplay content and the project's key terms. Music director Mitsukiyo said that roughly 80 percent of the new work's sonic direction differs from Blue Archive, using lo-fi and house as its foundation while placing comfort, retro sensibilities, and mystery in settings where everyday life and another world coexist; the team also added more live-instrument performances so the music could more readily evoke images of old houses, the countryside, and dusk. The unveiling therefore did more than fill in the game's name: it established a recognizable identity through both gameplay and atmosphere, allowing the previously scattered Project RX information to begin converging into a concrete work. That gives the anticipation something tangible to attach to.
