Bungie Reveals the Marathon Roadmap, Confirms the Addition of a Permanent PvE Mode (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

True Gaming تروجيمنج（沙烏地阿拉伯／泛阿拉伯地區，阿拉伯語） · 2026.09.28Original language: ar

Marathon's future direction will extend its survival competition across PvE, PvP, and PvEvP; the December "Symbiosis" major update will first add the game's first permanent PvE mode, while expanding Perimeter and adding new Shells, a social space, and a weapons/abilities testing area. Before that, the October update will first adjust progression, rewards, and the new-player experience; next March will add a deeper progression system, a remade Cradle, a story expansion, new enemies, and an entirely new area. Bungie says it will share more PvE details as Symbiosis nears release, and the roadmap as a whole places co-op and continued exploration within the long-term content development plan for survival competition.

Sakigarasu's Take

Permanent PvE keeps a way to play beyond the single competitive path in the game, letting players choose, depending on their mood, between confronting other players and dealing with the environment. But the roadmap is still a promise about the future; whether it lets players leave and return whenever they want without being bound by progression pressure will determine whether this option is a real expansion or another retention hook.
