The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Was Originally Planned to Include a Chain Whip, but the Technology Wasn't There at the Time (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

True Gaming تروجيمنج（沙烏地阿拉伯／泛阿拉伯地區，阿拉伯語） · 2026.09.13Original language: ar

The Songs of the Past expansion for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt will finally add the chain whip that was planned for the original version but postponed for years because of technical difficulties. Director Jakub Rokosz said the team dealt with the implementation obstacles from that time during the remake phase, while modern hardware also made it possible to realize this piece of equipment from Geralt's arsenal in the original work. It appeared in the official public showcase and has become one of the expansion's most eye-catching new pieces of equipment; the expansion is scheduled to launch next year, turning an early concept into new content that players will actually be able to use.
