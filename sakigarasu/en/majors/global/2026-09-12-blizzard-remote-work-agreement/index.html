After Two Years of Fighting, Blizzard Employees Secure an Agreement for Two Remote-Work Days per Week (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

JeuxVideo.com · 2026.09.11Original language: Français

After two years of negotiations, nearly 1,900 Blizzard employees ratified their first collective agreement, putting a hybrid system of three days in the office and two days working remotely each week into the contract. The company can no longer unilaterally change it to four or five days on site. The agreement also covers fully remote employees, disability-related accommodations, priority rehiring across studios within 14 months after layoffs, and consultation with employee representatives before introducing AI that affects working conditions.
