The Live-Action Street Fighter Actress Built Muscle to Recreate Chun-Li (translated)

To present Chun-Li's fighting style, which centers on leg techniques, in the live-action film, Karina Liang revealed in an interview with Empire that she trained in taekwondo, Muay Thai, and martial arts for two hours every day during filming, while also following a high-protein diet to build muscle. While gaining weight, she ate 12 eggs a day and had a meal every two hours; by the end of filming, each of her thighs had increased by about 6.5 centimeters. She also downloaded different versions of Street Fighter onto a PS5 and recorded Chun-Li's movements to study them in slow motion. The film is being produced by Legendary Entertainment and is scheduled for release on October 16, 2026.
