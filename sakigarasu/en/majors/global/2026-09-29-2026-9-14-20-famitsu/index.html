Famitsu’s weekly report on the Japanese market shows that the new Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave debuted at No. 1 on the game charts with 127,131 copies for its Switch 2 version in its first week, pushing weekly console sales above 34,000 units; that was still below the roughly 40,000 sold in the same period last year, but the Switch 2’s cumulative performance this year remains better than last year’s. The PS5, meanwhile, held at around 7,000 units per week, slightly below the roughly 8,000 in the same period last year. The charts also featured Switch 2 and PS5 versions of Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter, as well as new releases such as Marvel’s Wolverine, showing that both the hardware and software rankings were reshaped this week by a concentrated wave of launches.
