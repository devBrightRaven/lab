Sakigarasu's Take

Reworks and delays have given players back a rare but necessary choice: to step away and wait, rather than be bound by what they put in early on. The team is taking on the ambition of pursuing single-player, Online, and mods in parallel; that could expand the world, or make players spend their time plugging holes in the product. The first thing to watch is whether iteration gives players more power to shape the world; that matters more than whether the producers regret it.
