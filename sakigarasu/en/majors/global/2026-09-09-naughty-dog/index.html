Sakigarasu's Take

What game adaptations most easily lose is not a single character, but the scale of judgment players are familiar with; once the original work’s central creator steps out of the picture, viewers can only place their trust, for the time being, in the new team’s choices. There is nothing wrong with loving this world and being willing to wait for the next season, but this time the right worth preserving is the right to watch and see—not to endorse a show that has not yet aired.
