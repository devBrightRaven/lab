Romance of the Three Kingdoms XIV: Power-Up Legacy Edition launches, but some old save files cannot carry over (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

遊研社 · 2026.09.11Original language: 中文

Romance of the Three Kingdoms XIV with Power-Up Legacy Edition launched on PS5, PS4, Switch, and Steam on 10 September. It combines most of the DLC from the base game, Power-Up Kit, and two season passes, while adding systems such as military intelligence, merit, policies, and scenic sites. The new mechanics make intelligence resources affect diplomacy and the success rate of stratagems, and link officers’ positions to the development of their faction. However, old save files can only carry over new officers, edited data, and custom images; existing game progress cannot be continued directly, and PS4 saves cannot be inherited on PS5. The title was developed under the direction of Koei Tecmo in Tianjin, with the team retaining the original large-scale map and territorial-management pace while adding 190 historical events, 100 scenic sites, and further rule adjustments.

Players are paying not only the new price, but potentially the time required to start over; that is exactly why “complete inclusion” and “complete continuity” should not be treated as the same thing.
