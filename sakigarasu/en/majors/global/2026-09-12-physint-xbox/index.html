Physint Shifts to Xbox, Report Says Death Stranding Revenue Fell Short of Sony’s Expectations (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

4Gamers · 2026.09.12Original language: 中文

Physint was originally backed by Sony Interactive Entertainment, but later moved to a partnership with Xbox. Bloomberg, citing people familiar with the matter, reported that the split was driven in part by revenue from the Death Stranding series falling short of Sony’s expectations, as well as Physint falling behind schedule and going seriously over budget. The failure of Concord also led PlayStation to tighten its production schedules, further intensifying the pressure between the project and the platform. Because the later Xbox and PC versions of Death Stranding were not published by Sony, the company’s assessment of its investment costs and how it could recoup them also formed part of the background. Hideo Kojima’s upcoming OD and Physint are now both being funded and published by Xbox, with the agreement also including film and television adaptation rights.
