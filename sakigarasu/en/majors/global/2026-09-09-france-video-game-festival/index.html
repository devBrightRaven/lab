Macron Once Criticised Games, but Now Wants to Build an International Game Creation Festival in France (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

JeuxVideo.com · 2026.09.08Original language: Français

French President Emmanuel Macron announced plans to create an international game creation festival in Montpellier by 2027, positioning it as something akin to the Cannes Film Festival of the games industry, with a focus on creation, storytelling, technological innovation, players, and the industry. The initiative stands in sharp contrast to his position after the 2023 riots, when he criticised the influence of games; France’s game industry, meanwhile, is questioning whether this new cultural platform can address the financial crisis and structural problems facing studios.
