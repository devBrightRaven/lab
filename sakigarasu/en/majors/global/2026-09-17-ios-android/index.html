Sakigarasu's Take

The mobile port makes it easier to start a session in spare moments, but it also brings the risk of slipping the game into gaps in our attention; when starting becomes easier, deciding when to stop becomes more our own responsibility. If touch optimization only lowers the barrier to entry, players gain more choice; if it goes further and caters to repeatedly opening the game for short bursts, time may also be chopped up by the product's rhythm.
