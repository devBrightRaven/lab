Nexon Games to Debut a Playable Demo of Pareidolia at TGS 2026 (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

인벤 Inven（韓國，韓語） · 2026.09.17Original language: 한국어

Pareidolia will make its first appearance at the 2026 Tokyo Game Show as a playable 10-minute demo. Nexon Games has brought the game's core setting, the “Twilight Hall,” into physical form, letting players take on the role of the head of the restoration center and dormitory manager, build connections with the Meits, and deal with the city's problems. In the demo, a three-person party explores the Twilight Hall, which has become a dungeon due to an anomalous phenomenon; players predict enemy actions through real-time turn-based combat, adjust the Meits' action order, and use voice-recognition dialogue and character-calling features. The game will be exhibited at Makuhari Messe from September 17 to 21. RX Studio producer Cha Minseo said the team hopes to make the otherworld and the Meits feel more tangible while continuing to refine the game.

Sakigarasu's Take

The ten-minute demo breaks anticipation into pieces players can judge for themselves, so they do not have to decide whether to commit based solely on promotional language. If voice interaction becomes central, players should still have the option not to speak or to skip the interaction. Novelty can widen the entry point, but it cannot replace a choice about the long-term gameplay.
