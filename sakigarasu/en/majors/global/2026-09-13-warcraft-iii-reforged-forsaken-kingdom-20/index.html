Warcraft III Reforged launches Forsaken Kingdom: its first story campaign in 20 years is now live (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

DTF (игры) · 2026.09.12Original language: Русский

The new Forsaken Kingdom campaign for Warcraft III Reforged launched on Battle.net on September 12, 2026, marking the game's first new story campaign in 23 years. The story brings players back to Lordaeron and its surrounding areas: residents killed by the Scourge and resurrected as undead fight for their homeland under the leadership of Sylvanas Windrunner, while establishing the Undercity underground, surrounded by enemies. The campaign consists of a prologue and three chapters, runs for about 30 hours, adds an undead paladin hero, and is not restricted by which version of the strategy game the player owns. The expansion costs $30 on its own or $50 bundled with Warcraft III: Reforged; the Definitive Edition released alongside it also updates the visuals and map editor.
