Onimusha Sells Over One Million on Launch Day as Capcom Speeds Up Revival of Dormant IP (translated)

Onimusha: Way of the Sword sold more than 1 million copies worldwide on its launch day. The new entry in the series, which returned after an absence of more than 20 years, also helped push cumulative sales of the Onimusha series past 10 million copies. Capcom said the result shows that IPs without a new release for a long time may still have a market, so it will include its extensive library in its plans to reactivate dormant properties; other long-quiet series, such as Dino Crisis, have consequently drawn attention again.
