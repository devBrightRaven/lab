The article looks back at GTA cheat codes in the PS2 era. The author shares how they used familiar weapon codes to skip the intended progression, and how they and friends turned the game into a sandbox experiment: pushing the wanted level to its maximum and watching how the city responded. This way of playing could certainly cause players to miss Rockstar’s satirical storytelling, and it once deepened the wider moral panic around violence in games.
