A Parent's Guide to Marvel's Wolverine (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Policy · 2026.09.15Region: 美國Source: ESRB（美國官方分級）Original language: English

When evaluating Marvel’s Wolverine, parents should first separate the “superhero” premise from the content: the ESRB has rated the PlayStation 5 title M (17+), with rating descriptors covering blood and gore, intense violence, partial nudity, drug references, strong language, and in-game purchases. Combat is presented through claw attacks, stabbings, and slow-motion finishing moves, with dismemberment in some scenes as well; although Insomniac Games provides settings that can reduce the blood, dismemberment, and visible injuries to Wolverine, and can adjust the difficulty, language and drug-related content will not disappear as a result. In other words, what can be adjusted is some of the presentation of violence—not the M-rated content as a whole.

Sakigarasu's Take

The coexistence of an M rating and adjustable violence reminds parents and players to distinguish a boundary that is often blurred: visual intensity can be reduced, but responsibility for the content remains. For underage players, this adds options for negotiating together; for adult players, the rating still leaves room to keep the “superhero” label from making the judgment for them.
