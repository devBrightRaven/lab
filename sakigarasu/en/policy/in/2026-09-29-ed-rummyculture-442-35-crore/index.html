ED attaches assets worth Rs 442 crore in Rummyculture money laundering Case

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Policy · 2026.09.28Region: 印度Source: MediaNama（印度政策，英語，含遊戲）Original language: English

In the money-laundering investigation involving RummyCulture and related online gaming platforms, India’s Enforcement Directorate (ED) alleges that platforms linked to Gameskraft Technologies charged about 3 crore users a 10% to 15% commission on their bets, and used mechanisms such as bot players, withdrawal fees, and “Super Booster” to cause user losses or restrict withdrawals; the proceeds were then transferred to shareholders through dividends and share buybacks, before being hidden in trusts, funds, and high-value assets. ED’s latest provisional attachment covered assets worth 442.35 crore rupees linked to Gameskraft Technologies shareholders, including fixed deposits, commercial shops, villas, and residences; together with assets previously frozen, attached, and seized, the total is about 2,843 crore, and the case remains under investigation.
