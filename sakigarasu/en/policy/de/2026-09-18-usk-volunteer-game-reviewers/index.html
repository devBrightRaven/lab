Seeking Volunteer Game Reviewers (All Genders) en (translated)

Player agency: how policy constrains it, how the industry shapes it, how narrative design realizes it.

Policy · 2026.09.17Region: 德國Source: USK（德國官方分級機構）Original language: de

USK is recruiting players aged 18 or over who are familiar with different game genres and platforms to serve as volunteer Spielesichter*innen supporting the preparation of statutory age ratings. Those selected must thoroughly review submitted games, document their gameplay, technical features, story, and child- and youth-protection factors, write a test report, then present it to independent child-protection experts and answer their questions. The work requires being able to attend in person in Berlin and values reliability, confidentiality, communication skills, and German and English proficiency; each assignment is coordinated according to the number of cases, the available time, and the individual’s willingness, while the age rating itself is still determined through a formal review process.
